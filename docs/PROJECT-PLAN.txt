Deliver a final report (30%), a final presentation (10%) and evidence of individual contribution (15%) that answer The AM Network's brief on applying AI within a mining operation in a Papua New Guinea context. Target for every member: High Distinction.

----------------

POLICIES
--------
Team Charter V1_2 and Rules of Engagement V1_2 apply.

AI may support research, structure and language. It may not produce final engineering decisions, calculations, evaluations, references or conclusions without verification, and anything AI-derived needs a traditional source. All tools used go in the digital tool acknowledgment.

Files are named TX_DocumentName_VX_X. Submissions follow Marston_Tuesday_1530_Team3_descriptor. Actions are numbered Year-Meeting-Action, for example 2026-5-3.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES
------------------
1. Cover every body section (1 to 9), conclusion, references, tool acknowledgment and appendices to the page limits.
2. Show clearly why the recommended solution beats the alternatives, on cost and other named metrics.
3. Every section written by one person and edited by another, with both named.
4. One voice across the whole report.
5. Contribution logged weekly by every member, so the evidence is real and the ratings fair.

RISK MANAGEMENT
---------------
- Report reads as several authors
    Impact: High | Likelihood: High
    Language and presentation is 20 marks and asks for a single voice. Chief Editor does two full passes and keeps a short style sheet.
- A section has no independent editor
    Impact: High | Likelihood: Medium
    The brief requires every section to show who wrote it and who edited it. The Report page flags missing or identical names.
- AI-derived content or references not verified
    Impact: High | Likelihood: Medium
    Every AI-derived claim needs a traditional source. Check each reference exists. Fill in the digital tool acknowledgment.
- Solution is not shown to be the best
    Impact: High | Likelihood: Medium
    Quality of solution is 30 marks. Evaluation tables must compare options on cost and other metrics (NPV, ROI, payback).
- A teammate under-contributes
    Impact: High | Likelihood: Medium
    Marks scale by each person's contribution factor. Weekly ledger entries and a Tuesday check-in make it visible early.
- Late submission
    Impact: High | Likelihood: Low
    Internal target is Thu 22 Oct 8pm, one day before the deadline.
- Sections overrun page limits
    Impact: Medium | Likelihood: High
    Page budgets are on the Report page. Trim at the Wk 11 edit, not on submission night.
- System assumptions have no source
    Impact: Medium | Likelihood: Medium
    The assumptions table needs a source for each item. Link papers to sections in the Library.
- Meeting time slips after daylight saving starts (4 Oct)
    Impact: Low | Likelihood: Medium
    Confirm the Tuesday 21:00 time in the group chat in the week of 5 Oct.
- Leader overload (Team Leader and Chief Editor)
    Impact: Medium | Likelihood: Medium
    Delegate first-pass edits to the section editors. Keep Long's own writing to the executive summary, system integration and conclusion.
